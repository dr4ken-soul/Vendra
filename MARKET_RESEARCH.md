# Vendra Market and Competitor Research Notes

**Status:** Desk-research snapshot for planning, not a validated market study.  
**Language:** British English.  
**Important:** Public prices and product positioning can change. Recheck before publication or pricing decisions.

## 1. Beachhead market

Focus first on independent grocery/provisions retailers in one reachable Delta State market cluster. The founder can select a cluster based on access and recruitment; no specific city is assumed. The aim is to observe repeated supplier purchases, not to claim a statewide market size from national data.

### Hypothesised job to be done

“When I reorder from a supplier, show me what we agreed last time, what arrived and how any issue was resolved, with the record I can check.”

### Evidence to collect in discovery

- How retailer currently captures a supplier quote and the agreed terms.
- Where evidence lives: WhatsApp, call log, paper receipt, photo, spreadsheet or another tool.
- Whether a delivery mismatch occurred recently and how it was resolved.
- Time to retrieve a prior price/quantity and whether the person who remembers is present.
- Which facts the retailer is willing to store, who should access them and whether they would pay for recall.
- How wallet/account custody affects trust and onboarding.

## 2. Directional market-size proxy

The [SMEDAN/NBS 2021 MSME survey](https://www.nigerianstat.gov.ng/pdfuploads/MSMES.pdf) reports 39,654,385 MSMEs in Nigeria. A [2022 SMEDAN competitiveness report](https://fatefoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/7.-SMEDAN-1.pdf) describes wholesale and retail trade as approximately 33% of the MSME sector. Together these provide a rough broad-sector proxy of 13.1 million wholesale/retail MSMEs.

This proxy is not a count of independent grocery shops, repeat-purchase businesses, Delta retailers, smartphone-ready users or customers who will pay for Vendra. The following numbers are scenarios to test, not a forecast:

| Scenario | Formula | Result |
|---|---:|---:|
| Broad-sector proxy | 39,654,385 × 33% | ~13,085,947 businesses |
| Indicative TAM at ₦2,500/month | 13,085,947 × ₦2,500 × 12 | ~₦392.6bn/year theoretical ceiling |
| Assumed reachable SAM | 1% × 13,085,947 | ~130,859 businesses |
| Indicative SAM value | 130,859 × ₦2,500 × 12 | ~₦3.93bn/year scenario |
| Year-one founder-led SOM | 100 paying shops × ₦2,500 × 12 | ₦3m/year annualised target |

No Delta-specific sample frame or verified count of the chosen retailer type is present in this plan. The next step is to select a reachable cluster and build a real interview list.

## 3. Competitor and substitute view

| Product/substitute | Public positioning | Price signal observed | How Vendra differs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bumpa | Business management and commerce tools for Nigerian SMEs. | Public Starter plan at ₦15,000 quarterly and Pro at ₦30,000 quarterly, equivalent to ₦5,000/₦10,000 monthly before changes on the [Bumpa pricing page](https://www.getbumpa.com/pricing). | Vendra’s proposed wedge is an evidence-backed supplier-deal timeline and cross-session recall, not a broad store-management suite. |
| OmniRetail / OmniBiz / OmniStore | B2B commerce, retailer ordering, POS and distribution products. | No comparable public retailer SaaS price found in the [public product overview](https://blog.omnibiz.com/omnibiz-announces-a-brand-refresh/) reviewed. | Vendra does not sell or deliver goods. It is a private, retailer-scoped memory across supplier interactions; the implementation agent must select and verify the Walrus owner-account custody model. |
| Sabi | B2B commerce, marketplace and logistics infrastructure. | No comparable public per-retailer SaaS price found in the [Sabi overview](https://www.weforum.org/organizations/sabi/). | Vendra focuses on a shop’s private history across suppliers, not marketplace access. |
| Alerzoshop | Retail ordering and restocking app, tied to commerce and delivery. | Public app experience described as free; product/category availability can vary [on Alerzoshop’s site](https://alerzoshop.com/). | Vendra is not an ordering marketplace or delivery service. |
| Fisco | Storefront, sales, payment, order and inventory management. | Starter free with a 2.5% online-payment fee; Pro listed at ₦10,000/month on [Fisco’s public site](https://www.usefisco.com/). | Supplier-term recall with evidence is the proposed gap, not general sales administration. |
| WhatsApp, calls, paper and spreadsheets | Familiar, low-cost workarounds already embedded in retailer workflows. | Usually no separate SaaS subscription. | Vendra must prove it retrieves a specific deal faster and more reliably than searching existing records. |

These categories overlap and evolve. Public marketing copy cannot prove that another product lacks a feature. Describe Vendra’s differentiation as its proposed focus, not an absolute competitor claim.

## 4. Public review signal

[Individual comments on Bumpa’s App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bumpa-manage-orders-easily/id1497638594?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone) include positive notes on sales organisation and negative comments about login, paid-feature clarity and a payment settlement/KYC delay. Reviews are anecdotal and not representative. This plan does not assign unverified patterns of complaints to other local platforms.

## 5. Pricing hypotheses

- 30-day free pilot, no card, with onboarding and feedback.
- Solo: ₦2,500/month.
- Team: ₦7,500/month for up to five staff seats and one shared shop memory.
- Later multi-shop plan: ₦20,000/month for up to three shops, if there is demonstrated demand.

Test these against actual willingness to pay after retailers experience a successful later-session recall. Do not treat survey intent or a competitor’s subscription price as validation of Vendra’s price.

[Paystack’s public pricing page](https://paystack.com/pricing) lists 1.5% + ₦100 for local transactions, with a waiver of the ₦100 portion under ₦2,500 and a fee cap of ₦2,000. Verify current terms, subscriptions, refunds and settlement before billing.

## 6. Distribution hypothesis

Start founder-led with in-person observation and assisted onboarding in one Delta cluster. Ask each retailer to try one real recent deal, then return later to ask about it. Recruit through trusted business contacts and opt-in referrals. Use WhatsApp only for user-approved invitations or reminders. X, Product Hunt, Reddit and Indie Hackers may create developer visibility, but should not be the only route to the first retailers.
