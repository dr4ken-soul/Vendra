# Vendra Product Brief

## Product

**Name:** Vendra  
**Category:** Evidence-backed procurement memory for independent retailers  
**One-line pitch:** Vendra remembers what each supplier quoted, agreed, delivered and resolved, then brings the evidence back before the next purchase.

Vendra joins two ideas into one useful workflow: **MarketMemory**, which helps a retailer recall prior supplier deals, and **CaseProof**, which keeps the source records behind that recall. It is one deal lifecycle, not a bundle of unrelated business tools.

## The problem

A retailer may agree terms by phone or WhatsApp, receive goods later, then discover a quantity or quality issue. The quote, final agreement, delivery proof and resolution can be scattered across chat threads, paper slips and people’s memory. When the retailer orders again, they have to reconstruct what happened.

## The Vendra workflow

1. **Quote:** save what the supplier offered and attach the message, photo or note.
2. **Agreement:** record the final price, quantity and delivery terms.
3. **Delivery:** compare what arrived with what was agreed.
4. **Issue and resolution:** record a shortage, damage, delay or other discrepancy, plus the agreed outcome.
5. **Recall:** ask Vendra about the supplier or item later. The answer links back to the relevant deal events and evidence.
6. **Follow up:** use the sourced history to prepare a message. The retailer reviews and sends it themselves.

## First customer

Independent grocery and provisions retailers that make repeat purchases from several suppliers. Vendra should pilot in one accessible market cluster in **Delta State**, where the founder can speak directly with shop owners. The exact city and first market cluster remain a discovery decision. Expansion to other Delta clusters and then other Nigerian markets depends on repeat use, not on sign-ups alone.

## Product principles

- **Evidence before confidence:** answers point to saved records. If there is no relevant record, Vendra says so.
- **One shop, one private memory:** a retailer’s deals are never mixed with another retailer’s records.
- **The retailer controls sharing and actions:** Vendra recalls, organises and drafts. It does not place orders or send messages without the retailer. The implementation agent chooses and verifies the Walrus owner-account path, then discloses the real custody model before a pilot.
- **Keep the existing workflow:** Vendra should work alongside calls, paper and WhatsApp rather than require suppliers to join a new marketplace.
- **No invented proof:** no fabricated testimonials, savings figures, supplier ratings or market metrics.
- **No soundbox:** this is software for memory and evidence, not a physical payment or announcement device.

## V1 scope

- Capture a supplier, product, quote and agreed terms in a single deal record.
- Attach evidence and add delivery, issue and resolution events.
- Ask a conversational question and receive a source-linked answer from that shop’s history.
- Draft a supplier follow-up using the retailer’s selected records.
- Use Walrus Memory for durable, cross-session recall, with explicit account and tenant separation.

## Not in V1

Vendra is not a supplier marketplace, POS, stock-control system, lender, credit bureau, public supplier-rating network or autonomous purchasing agent. It does not expose one retailer’s deal history to another retailer or a supplier. Native mobile apps and multi-country support are later decisions.

## Walrus Session 8 fit

The attached challenge brief is **Walrus Session 8: Chatbots That Remember**. Vendra demonstrates that a chatbot can remember a supplier deal across separate conversations and use it to change the next conversation. The challenge requires a real deployment and use, at least three distinct users with at least ten memories stored for each, a public repository, an honest 500–800 word article and one X post. The post is planned in `MARKETING.md`; actual usage and evidence must be gathered before making claims.

## Success measures

The first success signal is not total records stored. It is a retailer returning to Vendra and using a recalled, evidence-linked fact before a later purchase or follow-up. Track:

- First deal created and evidence attached
- Successful recall in a later session
- Recall answers accepted or corrected by the retailer
- Weekly repeat use by the pilot shops
- Memory-sync failures and cross-shop access failures
- Paid intent and willingness to pay after the pilot

These are measurement definitions, not claimed results.
